## 1. What `L1DDER` Is in One Paragraph

**L1DDER** is a self-contained Spec-driven / AI-driven development framework (**Zero Hidden State**) designed so that:
1. **Humans** control the entire product lifecycle via **6 slash commands** (`/l1-onboard`, `/l1-idea`, `/l1-feature`, `/l1-planning`, `/l1-task`, `/l1-hotfix`) and `"ok"` approvals at `⛔ STOP` checkpoints.
2. **Any AI model and any agent (`Cursor`, `Claude Code`, `Codex`, `Windsurf`, `Copilot`, `OpenCode`, `Cline`)** works predictably thanks to linear step-by-step instructions, Few-Shot examples, and **traceable ID Passports** in every document header.
3. **Projects are initialized in 1 second via `npx l1dder init` (`--lang en|ru`)** with clean separation between universal `Core` rules and project-specific `Stack` rules.
